## Authentication methods

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Passkeys" icon="fingerprint">
    Device-bound credentials using WebAuthn. The strongest option — phishing-resistant and no password to steal.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email OTP" icon="envelope">
    One-time codes sent to the user's email. Good fallback for users without passkey-capable devices.
  </Card>
  <Card title="OAuth / Social" icon="brands-google">
    Sign in with Google, Apple, or other providers. Lowest friction for consumer apps.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
