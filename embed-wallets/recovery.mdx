## Recovery options

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Email recovery" icon="envelope">
    Send a time-limited recovery link to the user's email. They use it to register a new passkey.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Second authenticator" icon="mobile">
    Users can pre-register a second device. If they lose one, the other still works.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Admin recovery" icon="shield-check">
    Your backend initiates a recovery flow on behalf of the user, subject to your org's policies.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
